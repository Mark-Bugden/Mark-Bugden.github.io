I build machine learning models, and I care as much about understanding what a model has learned as about the predictions it makes. Before industry I was a theoretical physicist: a PhD in mathematical physics at the Australian National University, then postdoctoral research in Canberra, Prague and Konstanz on T-duality, generalised geometry and black holes. This site is home to projects I find interesting, each with an interactive demo and a write-up, and to my papers.
