I have always been interested in solving puzzles, especially one where finding the right approach is half the challenge. These days I work in machine learning, drawing on insights from physics-based intuition and mathematical modelling, alongside more traditional model training. Before moving into industry, I was a theoretical physicist. I did my PhD in mathematical physics at the Australian National University, followed by postdoctoral research positions in Canberra, Prague and Konstanz.

This site is a collection of my research papers and personal projects I've found particularly interesting.
