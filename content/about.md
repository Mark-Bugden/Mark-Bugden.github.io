I'm a data scientist with a background in theoretical physics, and I like getting to the heart of complex problems. I care about turning raw data into insight: not just predictions, but understanding.

I did my PhD in mathematical physics at the Australian National University and stayed on as an MSI “Kickstart” Postdoctoral Fellow, then held postdoctoral positions at Charles University in Prague and the Max Planck Institute in Konstanz. In 2023 I moved into applied machine learning as a Machine Learning Scientist at dida in Berlin, and since December 2025 I have been a Senior Data Scientist at Datamole in Prague.

My work spans classical machine learning and deep learning: XGBoost and scikit-learn, and neural networks in TensorFlow and PyTorch. I work mainly in Python, with pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn and Jupyter. I have taken models to production with Docker, served them as APIs with FastAPI, run them on Kubernetes, and automated delivery with GitLab CI/CD. For experiments I use MLflow for tracking and model versioning, and Hydra for configuration.
